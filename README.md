One image for the news of 24 November 2025: the day the peace plan got rewritten. Faceless diplomats sit around the Geneva negotiating table redrafting the document itself — "PEACE PLAN" with the big "28" crossed out in red, because after the Geneva talks the 28-point US plan had shrunk into a revised framework Zelenskyy said now held "many of the right elements"; one diplomat holds up Europe's counter-proposal, an hourglass drains beside the table for Trump's Thanksgiving deadline, and one chair stands empty under a placard reading "MOSCOW — STILL WAITING", because the Kremlin said that day it had not yet received the revised text. Behind them stand the big landmarks of the capitals involved: Geneva's Jet d'Eau, the US Capitol, Big Ben, the Eiffel Tower, the Brandenburg Gate, Kyiv's St. Sophia bell tower and St. Basil's Cathedral. Four corner vignettes carry the day's other stories: the abduction of 303 schoolchildren in Nigeria, Israel's strike on Beirut that killed Hezbollah's chief of staff, the United States designating Venezuela's Cartel of the Suns a terrorist organization as its warships massed in the Caribbean, and Brazil's Supreme Court keeping Jair Bolsonaro in custody. The program in `main.ts` simply copies `entry.png` to the output directory; the image is the entry.
